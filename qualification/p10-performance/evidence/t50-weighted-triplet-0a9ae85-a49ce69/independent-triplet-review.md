Signed mean differences were less than 0.035 codes on each RGB channel. None of the three outputs contained a channel at the u16 endpoints. [Independent image metrics](independent-image-audit.json)

The 50 original depth windows were compared at their common physical coordinates after accounting for the one-pixel crop. Their pairwise MAE ranged from 8.357 to 9.911 u16 codes. The resident/reference vertical second-difference variance ratio over RGB channels was 1.000372 to 1.000453. Equal-tone displays of eight evenly spaced depth windows at the same nearest-neighbor scale showed the same edge placement, fine texture and color. The amplified residual displayed small structured differences around oblique fine edges. The whole-canvas overview showed no gross geometry, tone or color discrepancy. The pinned truth receipt and all 50 depth coordinates match the archived generator records. The reported whole-image sharp-truth MAE is 1083.931 for ChimpStackr and 1080.736 for either Pancake arm. The retained oracle's grayscale detail ratios relative to sharp truth are about 0.8405–0.8825 for all three. This review did not reopen sharp truth or original input TIFFs. Those absolute truth metrics are from the archived runner, while the pairwise metrics are independently recomputed from the exports.

The typed 18-member packet matched its digest and contained the same result as the original typed report. The frozen targets, tool hashes, generated source receipt, corpus custody record, and producer revision/tree/lock fields matched their expected identities.
